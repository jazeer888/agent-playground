# Lumen Audio: support knowledge

Lumen Audio is a small (fictional) online store that sells wireless
headphones and earbuds. Everything the agent may state about the company is
on this page. If it is not here, the agent must not invent it.

## Products

| Product | Price | Battery | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lumen Go (earbuds) | $79 | 7 h, 28 h with case | IPX4 splash resistant |
| Lumen Pro (over-ear) | $199 | 40 h | Active noise cancelling |
| Lumen Studio (over-ear, wired + wireless) | $299 | 60 h | Includes 3.5 mm cable |

## Orders and shipping

- Free standard shipping on orders over $50; otherwise $6.
- Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express (2 days) is $15.
- Tracking links are emailed when the order ships. Customers can also find
  them under **My orders** after signing in on the website.
- The agent cannot look up a specific order. For order status, ask the
  customer to check **My orders** or to email support with the order number.

## Returns and refunds

- 30-day return window from the delivery date, any reason.
- Items must be in original condition with all accessories.
- Refunds go to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of
  receiving the return.
- Start a return from **My orders > Return item**. A prepaid label is emailed.

## Warranty

- 2-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects.
- Not covered: water damage beyond the stated rating, accidental damage,
  normal wear of ear pads and tips.
- Warranty claims: email support with the order number and a short
  description (photos help).

## Common fixes

- **Won't pair:** hold the power button 8 seconds until the light flashes
  blue and red (pairing mode), then forget the device on the phone and pair
  again.
- **One side silent (Lumen Go):** put both earbuds in the case, close it for
  10 seconds, open it and pair again.
- **Firmware updates:** through the Lumen Audio app (iOS and Android), under
  Settings > Firmware.

## Contact and hours

- Email: support@lumenaudio.example (replies within one business day)
- Live chat on the website: Monday to Friday, 9:00-18:00 CET
- No phone support.

## Payment

Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal and Apple Pay. Payment details are
only ever entered on the checkout page. Support staff never ask for card
numbers, passwords or one-time codes.
